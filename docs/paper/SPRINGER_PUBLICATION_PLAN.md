**Recommended strategy:**

**One paper only (CHAT-33).** Scope, claim strength and page budget: [THE_ONE_PAPER](THE_ONE_PAPER.md). The venue (journal such as *SN Computer Science* or *Agricultural Research*, or a Springer proceedings conference) is chosen with the supervisor, because it decides how much of the work fits in the main text.

Do not split one study into overlapping papers; each must report distinct results.

## 3. Comparison with prior work (how "peers" are compared)

EXP-00 builds a structured literature table with 20–40 papers. Each paper is recorded with:

- **Identity:** citation and year.
- **Setting:** country/region; crops.
- **Data:** source and period.
- **Methods:** forecasting method(s); whether it optimises allocation (and how); fairness handled?; storage/IoT used?; farmer communication?; human approval step?
- **Evaluation:** real-data evaluation?; baselines used?; statistical tests?; open code/data?
- **Limitations noted.**

From this table the paper shows:

1. A **feature comparison matrix**: KisanNiti versus representative prior systems on the dimensions above, marking only what each paper actually reports.
2. A **results comparison** only where targets match (same crop, market type and horizon). Otherwise the paper states that direct numeric comparison is not valid.
3. A **novelty statement** derived from the gaps found, never assumed in advance.

Literature sources: Springer Link, ScienceDirect, IEEE Xplore, Google Scholar, and Indian agricultural economics journals. Record the search strings, dates and inclusion criteria so the review is reproducible.

## 5. Risks to publication

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| No real data access | Register early (USER_ACTIONS B1/B2) |
| Weak or negative results | Report honestly; negative findings, such as "advice does not beat selling at harvest in most seasons", are still publishable when the study is rigorous |
| No ethics approval for human studies | Limit human studies to what is approved |
| Synthetic organisation data | State it clearly as a limitation; the evaluated parts (forecasting, backtest on prices) use real data |
| Predatory venues | Only Springer titles verified on link.springer.com |
